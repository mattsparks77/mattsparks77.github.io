Two ways to play

Take your time, or don't

🌍

Story

Work through cities one at a time, three stars per level. Clear a country and the next stamp lands on the board.

🔥

Survival

Three hearts, no timer mercy. Litter keeps coming until you miss one too many. Chase the streak.

✨

Combos

Three or more identical items touching? Tap the group and clear them all at once. The board rewards noticing.
